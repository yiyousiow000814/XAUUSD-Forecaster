# Storyline Promotion Protocol

Storyline state remains display-only unless a separate research campaign proves
that its event assignment is reliable and incrementally useful.

## Procedure

1. Manually review story assignment, duplicate handling, confirmations,
   contradictions, revisions, and event-time ordering.
2. Freeze a new versioned feature schema without rewriting historical evidence.
3. Evaluate the candidate forward-only against the same-clock Market-only
   control.
4. Report incremental quote-cost-adjusted value and uncertainty separately from
   presentation quality.
5. Keep the candidate Shadow-only unless the owner completes the ordinary
   manual promotion process.

The procedure cannot relax the evidence and causality guarantees in
[`FORWARD_ONLY.md`](../contracts/FORWARD_ONLY.md) or
[`NEWS_EVIDENCE.md`](../contracts/NEWS_EVIDENCE.md).
