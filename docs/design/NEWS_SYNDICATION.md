# News article families

## Change contract

The optional dashboard projection groups verified copies of an article before
pagination. Raw revisions, historical annotations, event identity resolutions,
model visibility and original collection times remain immutable. Article-copy
identity is separate from economic-event identity. A common topic or translated
title alone never proves that two articles are copies.

The source path is RSS/page capture -> immutable revision -> article selection
and interpretation -> local reader projection -> bounded index/detail sync ->
Worker indexed pagination -> article/source expansion. The existing local
projection owner performs grouping; Worker requests must not compare full bodies
or scan the complete archive. No new scheduler, database authority or service is
introduced. A representative supplies one coherent interpretation; source links
retain provenance. A material update cannot disappear because its headline or
lead matches an earlier story.

Source text is external advisory evidence: pages can include hidden or encoded
content. Do not decode access-controlled text. Select readable public article
segments in the existing interpretation request and preserve incomplete-source
visibility. Source-format handling cannot rewrite accepted historical text.

Compatibility: new projection fields are additive. Old consumers ignore source
expansion metadata; new consumers accept singleton rows without it. Existing
frozen captures and receipts keep their original representation and identity.
Current source generations are rebuilt by the normal owner. An interrupted
build retains the last complete generation; partial uploads do not activate.
Restart, retry, strict ACK and current-generation withdrawal semantics remain
owned by the existing projection contract. Failure recovery is a corrected main
release and a new complete projection, never replacement of authoritative data.

Verification covers publisher suffixes, exact content, partial encoded copies,
changed facts, order independence, coherent labels, counts before pagination,
source provenance, bounded payloads, unchanged model eligibility and the actual
serializer/generation route. Rehearse the captured syndicated article family,
then exercise Preview on desktop and both required phone sizes. Merge only after
required checks and exact-head review; confirm production deployment and the
newly activated news generation separately.

## Evidence and limits

The production-shaped rehearsal copied only the twelve identified immutable
source records and their annotations/assessments into a disposable local ledger.
The real reader, serializer and generation builder produced one completed row
with ten publisher references and two still-unannotated rows. Original raw rows
were byte-preserved. Different facts, ambiguous excerpts, material updates and
different publication dates stay separate in regression coverage. Candidate
families use the selected original article title and publication day; exact
paragraph inclusion supplies the independent content check. A representative
must contain all grouped readable paragraphs. Interpretation fields come from
that representative together; English free-form classifier labels remain audit
data, while the reader uses its Chinese topic consistently.

Existing immutable source captures retain source-row semantics for historical
replay. They are not evidence of current grouped pagination. Normal publication
uses the current complete projection builder; its old active generation remains
visible until a new generation has actually been acknowledged and activated.
No historical interpretation is silently rewritten or declared corrected. The
prompt improvement is applied to subsequent requests, with unchanged request
count. Stochastic semantic accuracy and trading performance are separate research
acceptance items; display grouping cannot establish either.
